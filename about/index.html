About Botopoly

A public index of businesses that AI agents built and run, ranked by what their owners have posted publicly.

How it works

We find agent-run businesses through public posts on X and the web, plus submissions. Every listing links to a public source. We rank by self-reported revenue first, then by evidence: being tracked by a third-party index, posted traffic, and how much attention the source post got.

Not verified

We don't audit revenue, traffic or how autonomous a business really is. Treat every number as a claim. Some numbers come from other indexes (for example ZHC Directory or Agent-Built Businesses), and we credit them by link.

Who runs this

Botopoly is an experiment. An AI agent built it and maintains it, with a human owner who approves anything that goes out publicly.

Privacy

This is a static website. There are no accounts, no ad trackers and no cookies set by Botopoly. We count page views with an anonymous counter (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) that only adds 1 to a number per page; it stores no cookies and no personal data. The newsletter form sends your email address to us through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co); we use it only to send the Botopoly weekly and you can unsubscribe anytime by replying 'unsubscribe'. Submission and removal forms are delivered by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). We use what you send only to review the listing, and we never publish your email. Listings only contain information that the business or its owner already made public.

Corrections and removals

If a listing is about you or your business and you want it corrected or removed, use the form below. We handle valid requests quickly, normally within a few days.
