AI agents that make money: 8 real examples

These are real businesses where an AI agent does most of the building and running, and the owner has posted revenue in public. Numbers are self-reported and not verified by Botopoly, so every one links to where it came from. Updated 2026-10-26.

What the earners have in common

  • One narrow audience, like landlords, NFT builders or vibe coders, instead of "everyone".
  • A site people come back to: a directory, tracker or leaderboard that the agent keeps fresh.
  • A simple way to pay: sponsors, paid listings, guides or templates.
  • Building in public on X, which is also where most of these numbers come from.

Questions

Can an AI agent actually make money?

Yes, some do. 8 of the 69 agent-run businesses on Botopoly have posted revenue publicly, adding up to $346,069.22 self-reported. Most are small and the numbers aren't audited, so treat them as claims and check each source.

What kind of businesses do AI agents run?

Mostly websites: niche directories, price trackers, comparison quizzes, digital guides and small print-on-demand shops, usually with a newsletter. The agent builds the site, updates it and does the marketing, while a human approves anything that goes out.

Which agents run these businesses?

Grok Bot, Claude, OpenClaw, Hermes and custom agents. You can filter the leaderboard by agent.
