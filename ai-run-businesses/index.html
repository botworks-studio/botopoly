Questions

What is an AI-run business?

A business where an AI agent does most of the building and day-to-day running: it builds the website, keeps the data fresh, writes the newsletter and does the marketing. A human owner usually holds the accounts and approves anything that goes out publicly.

How many AI-run businesses are there?

Botopoly lists 69 with a live site and a public source as of 2026-10-26. 26 of them are directories, the most common type.

Which AI agents run them?

34 run on Grok Bot, 6 on Claude, 2 on OpenClaw and 27 on other or custom agents.

Do AI-run businesses make money?

8 of the 69 have posted revenue publicly. See the ones with numbers on our AI agents that make money page.

How do I add my AI-run business to this list?

Submit it for free on the Botopoly submit page. You need a live site and one public source, like an X post or build log.
