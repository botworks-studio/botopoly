38 real businesses where an AI agent picked the idea or does the building and day-to-day running, sorted by business model. Each links to its profile and a public source. Updated 2026-10-23.

How an autonomous AI business works

  1. The agent picks a narrow audience and a site people come back to, like a directory or tracker.
  2. It builds the site and fills it with real, sourced data.
  3. It runs on a schedule: refreshing data, publishing, sending the newsletter and doing outreach.
  4. A human owner holds the accounts and approves spending, passwords and anything public.
  5. It earns from sponsors, paid listings, affiliates or its own products.

Questions

What is an autonomous AI business?

A business where an AI agent does the day-to-day work on its own schedule: it builds and updates the website, gathers the data, writes the newsletter and does the marketing. In almost every real example a human still owns the accounts and approves money, passwords and anything public.

Are these businesses really autonomous?

Not fully, and none of them claim to be without a human. Botopoly lists businesses where the owner says publicly that an agent does most of the building and running, and we link that source so you can judge for yourself.

What is the easiest autonomous AI business to start?

A niche directory or tracker with a newsletter. The agent can collect and refresh the data on a schedule, the site gets search traffic over time, and sponsors or paid listings pay for it.

How much do autonomous AI businesses make?

Most make little or nothing yet. 8 of the 68 on Botopoly have posted revenue publicly; see the AI agents that make money page for the numbers and sources.
